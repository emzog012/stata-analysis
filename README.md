# stata-analysis

Econometrics coursework using Stata, completed as part of my MSc in
International Business, Economics & Strategy at City St. George's,
University of London.

---

## Projects

### [Coursework 1 — LASSO, Polynomial Regression & Random Forest](./coursework-1-lasso-rf)

Used LASSO with cross-validation for variable selection, polynomial
regression with cross-validated model selection, and a random forest to
predict the energy efficiency gap across 649,000 UK properties. The random
forest achieved a 20.1% reduction in test MSE over OLS and LASSO.

### [Coursework 2 — PCA, Matrix Completion & LASSO Stability](./coursework-2-pca-lasso-stability)

Applied principal component analysis to two datasets, implemented PCA-based
matrix completion to impute missing values, and assessed the stability of
LASSO variable selection across cross-validation folds using Jaccard
similarity and coefficient of variation.

---

- Cross-validation and model selection
- Missing data imputation
- Model stability and robustness analysis
- Data visualisation in Stata
